🎮 How to Play

You are an Eco Ranger. Clean ALL the litter in each area before the timer runs out, recycle it into the correct bins, and rescue trapped animals. Every piece you recycle heals the world — watch it transform!

Controls

WASDMove (Arrow keys work too)
MouseLook around & aim the crosshair
SPACEJump — press again mid-air to Double Jump
SHIFTSprint
CLICKGrapple! Hold on a ⭕ ring to swing, release to fly. Click glowing litter to yank it into your bag
EInteract — rescue animals, recycle your bag at the station
VHold for Future Vision — preview the healed world
PPause

Tips

• Your bag holds 12 items — empty it at the ♻ Recycling Station (press E) for bonus points.
• Chain pickups fast to build an Eco Combo — up to ×10 score!
• Litter on rooftops or floating on water? Grapple it from a distance.
• Grab power-ups: 🧲 Magnet · 👟 Speed · ❄ Freeze Time · ⭐ Double Score.
• Find the hidden 🏆 Golden Bottle in every level!
